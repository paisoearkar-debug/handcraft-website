ABOUT HANDCRAFT

Crafting Spaces, Building Futures

Handcraft Myanmar Co., Ltd. is a Myanmar-based construction, interior design, renovation and custom furniture company dedicated to creating functional, refined and enduring spaces.

01 / OUR VISION

Design with purpose. Build with integrity.

To be an efficient and trusted Design & Build company, operating with integrity and balance — creating spaces our clients are proud of and building a company where our employees are proud to belong.

02 / OUR MISSION

Quality in every detail.

To deliver quality workmanship, thoughtful design and exceptional customer service while maintaining the highest standards of professionalism, honesty and fairness with our clients, employees, partners and suppliers.

03 / OUR CORE VALUES

What guides our work.

04 / PEOPLE FIRST
